# Jump and Run

A small 2.5D platformer the user is building to **learn Unity**. When you change code, explain the Unity idea behind it (for example interpolation or why physics runs in FixedUpdate). Don't just make silent fixes.

## Working mode
- **Never assume. Always ask, interview style.** Before you implement or decide anything (design, gameplay, code structure, naming, assets), ask the user with a set of concrete options (use the AskUserQuestion tool). Put the option you **recommend first** and mark it as recommended. Only act once the user has chosen.
- **Git**: local repo (branch `main`, no remote, no LFS yet; set up LFS before the first large binary like models/textures/audio). **Commit after every finished work item**, as soon as it compiles cleanly and is done on your side, without waiting for the playtest. Tuning or fixes after the playtest get their own small commits. Commit messages in **English**, short and imperative. `.gitignore` covers `Library/`, `Temp/`, `Logs/`, `UserSettings/`, `obj/`, `*.csproj`, `*.slnx`, `.DS_Store`.

## Theme (decided as of 2026-10-23)
Only decided things go here, no loose ideas.
- **Pizza delivery**: the player is a pizza delivery guy carrying a pizza carton. Each level he picks up a new pizza at his **pizzeria** (start, a house in the background) and delivers it to a **customer** (goal), who is super happy and starts dancing.
- **Level 1 = city.** Enemies are dogs (instead of the red `Enemy`). Platforms are city objects (benches, balconies, ...).
- **Heat meter + tip**: the pizza cools down over time (a heat bar at the top center, no seconds shown). The remaining heat on delivery sets the tip as **1–3 gold coins** (heat thirds: ≥ 2/3 = 3, ≥ 1/3 = 2, > 0 = 1). At 0: "Pizza's cold!" for 1.5 s, then the level restarts. Respawning costs no extra heat (the lost time is the penalty). The TimeBonus power-up warms the pizza back up and **may go above 100%** (buffer, shown as a pulsing overlay on the bar). Keep-warm time: level 1 = 60 s, level 2 = 80 s (scene overrides on `levelTime`, still to be tuned).
- **Delivery**: player frozen (input off, gravity/platform keep going), panel "Delivered!" + coins, Jump continues to the next level.
- **Steam** on the carton fades evenly with the heat.
- **Customer** (goal): lives in their own **customer house** (house no. 42, part of the Goal prefab, integrated into the row of buildings). They wait inside and come out of the door once the player is < 8 m away, then sway and wave (< 6 m). After a respawn they go back inside (unless the pizza was already delivered). On delivery the carton hops over in an arc into their hands, then they dance **by tip**: 1 coin = sway, 2 = bounce + alternating arms, 3 = hop + spin + arms overhead. Shirt/skin color per level in the Inspector. The "Delivered!" panel is compact at the top (no darkening), so the dance stays visible.
- **Dog**: walks back and forth (1.7 m/s) and turns at ledges and walls; **never falls off a ledge** (when chasing it stops there). It **sees** the player only in its viewing direction (6 m, ±1.5 m height, free line of sight); sneaking up from behind works. Once spotted: stop, "WOOF!" bubble + hop 0.5 s (warning, again every time it spots you), then charges at 5 m/s (player 7; at 6 you landed on its head almost every time you jumped past). While chasing it keeps the player in view in all directions and gives up after 1.5 s without sight. Fur/ear colors per dog in the Inspector.
- **Street with cars**: `Street Crossing` is an overlay on a sidewalk, a **real side street** in a 10 m gap of the building row: road at road level (0.3) with sidewalks + curbs on both sides up to z = 104 (into the fog), crossing the main road in front (yellow line covered), and a **raised crosswalk** at sidewalk height (0.5) with 1.5 m ramps on the walk lane, so the lane stays flat. Cars come in a fixed rhythm from the background (+Z, spawn at 100 m; was 40, +60 m = exactly 2 intervals, so the rhythm at the crosswalk is unchanged) toward the camera and roll over the bump (`heightOverZ` curve); the warning is that you see them coming. Default 12 m/s, every 2.5 s, lane 2 offset by 1.25 s. **The whole car is deadly** (respawn), jumping all the way over it is allowed. On respawn every lane starts its rhythm from the beginning. In level 1 at u = 50 (`CityLevelBuilder.StreetU`).
- In-game texts in **English**.
- **Level 1 = city street with a 90° bend after the coffee shop** (as of 2026-10-23, generated by `CityLevelBuilder`, about 170 m, keep-warm time 75 s): sidewalk with road and curb in front, buildings behind (facades at Z = 3), skyline in the fog, clouds. Gaps = **construction pits** (4 m deep, the KillZone sits right above the floor at y = -3, so the death is still visible). Order: pizzeria (start) → balcony climb + speed boost → pit → dog between two dumpsters → cross street with traffic → **coffee shop = checkpoint** → bouncy awning → high balcony with warm-up bonus → long pit over the window-washer gondola and two balconies → 2 dogs (bench/dumpsters as turning walls) + balcony with jump boost → customer.
- **Start/respawn from the door**: at the level start the delivery guy walks out of the pizzeria. **Death** (dog, car, pit) = freeze, squash, spin, puff of smoke; then the level resets and he walks out of the door of the last building (pizzeria, or coffee shop after the checkpoint). Further hits during that time don't count.
- **Checkpoint feedback (coffee shop)**: before = shop dark (windows off, sign dim, "CLOSED" on the door and on the sidewalk chalkboard, floating coffee cup as a hint). When reached: lights on, the "OPEN" neon flickers on, the chalkboard flips to "OPEN – fresh coffee", the cup pops with a sparkle burst, and **"Checkpoint!"** shows in the HUD for about 1.5 s.
- **Stopwatch**: delivery time (mm:ss.s, counting up) next to the heat bar and on the "Delivered!" panel. Heat = how hot, time = how fast.
- **Microwave** (24/7 kiosk at u = 80, after the bend, before the long pit): walking into it automatically walks the player to the counter, the carton goes into the microwave, it runs (glowing glass, countdown, turntable), "DING!", carton out, back onto the lane, **heat back to 100%** (a buffer above that stays). Once per life (display READY → USED, back to READY after a respawn). Costs about 2.5 s on the stopwatch: hot pizza vs. speed.
- **Bouncy awning**: shop awning as a trampoline (~5 m high, independent of the jump button). The window-washer gondola works as an elevator (6 m).
- **Dog stomp**: no squashing. The player still bounces, the dog gets dizzy (stars over its head, 1 s), runs toward the camera (−Z, 10 m, small hop down the curb onto the road) out of the bottom of the screen and disappears, then comes back on `LevelReset`.
- **Placeholder look**: until there's a real model, the player stays a **sphere** (collider radius 0.5; physics unchanged) and carries a red carton with a white lid (`Pizza Carton` → `Box`/`Lid`, materials `PizzaRed`/`PizzaWhite`, no colliders).

## Setup
- Unity **6000.6.2f1** (Unity 6), URP 17.6, new **Input System** 1.20 (read directly with `Keyboard.current` / `Gamepad.current`, so the `InputSystem_Actions.inputactions` asset isn't wired up yet).
- Code comments, Inspector headers and tooltips are in **German**. Keep it that way.
- Scenes: `Assets/Scenes/levels/level01.unity`, `level02.unity` (`SampleScene` is the template leftover).
- Physics: 50 Hz fixed timestep (0.02), default gravity -9.81. The player turns off Rigidbody gravity and applies its own.
- Layer 3 = `Ground`, the player's `groundLayer` for ground checks. The player has the `Player` tag.
- **Unity MCP** (official, package `com.unity.ai.assistant` 2.20.0-pre.2, pre-release; registered in `.mcp.json`): `Unity_RunCommand` runs C# in the open editor (class `internal class CommandScript : IRunCommand`; `System.Reflection` is blocked; `result.Log` doesn't understand format specifiers, so use `$"{x:F2}"`), `Unity_GetConsoleLogs`, camera/scene captures (they don't include a Screen Space Overlay UI). While Unity recompiles, the bridge drops out briefly ("Unity not detected"), so wait and try again.
- **Enter Play Mode Options are on** (Domain Reload + Scene Reload disabled): statics survive between Play sessions (e.g. `GameManager.Instance` points to the old object after Stop, and static events keep listeners that aren't removed in `OnDestroy`). After Stop, don't read values through `Instance`.
- Screenshots of the game camera via the MCP: render `Camera.main` into a RenderTexture in `RunCommand`, save it as a PNG under `Temp/` and read it (`Unity_Camera_Capture` without an ID only shows the Scene View; `GetInstanceID()` is a compile error in 6.6, it's `GetEntityId()` now). The **relay shuts down after 180 s without MCP activity**; after that, calls hang and the user has to run `/mcp` → Reconnect.
- The MCP rejects `AssetDatabase.DeleteAsset` ("User interactions are not supported"): delete assets with `git rm` (+ .meta) instead, and Unity picks it up on refresh.
- Teleporting during the intro/respawn sequence doesn't work (the player is kinematic and `PlayExit` moves him back). Wait until `GameManager.Instance.IsRespawning` is false first. `Time.timeScale` for slow motion is runtime only, but set it back to 1 anyway.
- Play mode tests through the MCP take several seconds per call: **level 1 goes cold after 60 s** and the level restarts (looks like a mysterious respawn). Run `GameManager.Instance.AddTime(600)` first in the test.
- **Input in MCP tests**: `InputSystem.QueueStateEvent(Keyboard.current, new KeyboardState(Key.D))` holds a key (`new KeyboardState()` releases it). That only works if `InputSystem.settings.editorInputBehaviorInPlayMode = AllDeviceInputAlwaysGoesToGameView` and `backgroundBehavior = IgnoreFocus` are set (in-memory only, no settings file in the project; reset afterwards). Play mode can also end in the meantime: check `EditorApplication.isPlaying` before modifying, or you're changing the scene in edit mode.
- Play mode tests through the MCP: the editor usually isn't focused, so frames won't advance unless `Application.runInBackground = true`. **Caution: in the editor this writes `PlayerSettings.runInBackground` into `ProjectSettings.asset` (persistent!).** After testing, reset it with `PlayerSettings.runInBackground = false; AssetDatabase.SaveAssets();` (the project value is `false`) and check `git diff ProjectSettings/`.
- TextMeshPro Essential Resources are imported (`Assets/TextMesh Pro/`).

## Gameplay structure
- **2.5D on a lane path**: the walk lane is a `LanePath` (straight pieces + arcs in the XZ plane, lane coordinate **u**; on the first straight piece along +X, u = world X). **Level 1 bends 90° into the depth after the coffee shop** (left turn toward +Z, radius 4 m, u 64–70.3). Lane frame at u: local +X = walking direction, +Z = "back" (building side), −Z = camera side; `Lane.Frame(u)`. All city prefabs are built in this frame and placed rotated. **Without a LanePath (level 2), `Lane` behaves exactly like the old X-axis at Z = 0.** Player rotation is frozen for physics (constraints 112), position Z is **no longer** frozen (the lane holds it). `PrefabGridEnforcer` (editor) snaps prefab roots **along the lane** (u and y to whole meters, rotation = lane frame; UI/RectTransforms excluded; without a lane, the old X/Y grid at Z = 0).
- `PlayerController`: Rigidbody movement (velocity set directly). **Input x = forward/back along the lane** (no longer camera-relative; W/S don't move); each `FixedUpdate` `SnapToLane(u)` projects the player back onto the path (sideways correction) and sets `body.rotation = Lane.Frame(u)` (so the carton sits on the correct side in local X); sideways velocity is dropped.  Celeste/Hollow-Knight-style jump: gravity derived from `jumpHeight`/`timeToApex`, stronger gravity when falling, jump cut on release, apex hang, coyote time and jump buffer. Powerups call `ApplySpeedBoost` / `ApplyJumpBoost` (the latter multiplies jump velocity).
- `ThirdPersonCamera`: follows the player in `LateUpdate`; offset (0, 5, −8) and viewing angle **in the lane frame at the player's position** (in a curve it swings around the outside), separate SmoothDamp for horizontal/vertical, rotation smoothed with `rotationSharpness` (exponential).
- `PointToPointMover`: the shared A↔B movement (used directly by the **Flying Enemy** prefab). `MovingPlatform` is an empty subclass of it, so the player recognizes platforms. Movement details:
- `MovingPlatform`: kinematic Rigidbody, moves A↔B with ease in/out (`easingStrength`, peak speed = `speed` × strength; `speed` = average speed), waits `waitTime` at each end, starts from its editor position (projected onto A→B, heading toward B first), and only waits at start if it sits on an endpoint. A/B are children of the platform (`moving platform start/end`) and their positions are read once in `Start`. It does **not** move passengers itself; it exposes `Velocity`, and `PlayerController` adds that velocity to its own (`carryVelocity` stays while airborne, so jumps keep platform momentum; rising platforms add their upward speed to the jump). Never move the dynamic player with `MovePosition`/teleporting, because that fights the physics and kills jumps.
- `PowerUpShine` (on the Player prefab) + `Assets/Shaders/PowerUpShine.shader` (hand-written URP HLSL, additive fresnel rim + sweeping stripe): while a boost is active, it attaches the shine material as an extra material on the renderer, colored per power-up type, and blinks shortly before the boost runs out. `PlayerController.ApplySpeedBoost/ApplyJumpBoost` trigger it.
- Prefabs are capitalized (`Platform Big`, `Moving Platform Small`, `Dog`, `Checkpoint`, `Goal`, `PowerUp`, ...). Paths in editor scripts must match exactly.
- Enemy prefabs: `Dog` (formerly `Enemy`, renamed with the same GUID; `GroundPatrol` + `Dog`, BoxCollider 1.25 × 0.89 m) and `Flying Enemy` (purple, `PointToPointMover` between its children `Point A`/`Point B`). Both use `EnemyDamage`.
- `EnemyDamage`: a contact from above (direction to player `y >= stompDirectionThreshold`, player not rising) is a **stomp**. Stomp is decided **by height**: the player's underside ≥ the enemy collider's top edge − `stompHeightTolerance` (0.2), for all enemies. The enemy is disabled immediately (collider plus all other scripts), and `PlayerController.StompBounce()` bounces the player (higher while jump is held). If the enemy has a component with **`IStompHandler`** (e.g. `Dog`), it stays enabled and decides the reaction itself in `OnStomped()` (and has to call `SetActive(false)` at the end); otherwise it squashes flat. Any other contact calls `GameManager.RespawnPlayer()`.
- `GroundPatrol` (replaces the old waypoint patrol `EnemyPlatformPatrol`; the platform prefabs no longer have `WaypointA/B` children): kinematic Rigidbody, moves **along the lane** (own `u`, fixed height, `MovePosition(Lane.Point(u))` + `MoveRotation(Lane.Frame(u))`, so also through curves). Ledge check = raycast down at `Lane.Point(u ± (half body length + lookahead))` (body length from the BoxCollider size, not from the axis-aligned bounds), wall check = raycast along the tangent. Vision: distance **along the lane** (`sightRange`) + height (`sightHeight`), viewing direction only to spot, `Physics.Linecast` against `sightBlockers` for line of sight, `loseSightTime`. Hooks: `IsChasing`, `HoldPosition` (stand still, keep facing the player), `FacingDirection`, `flipTarget` (for the dog `Body`, so collider/Rigidbody stay unmirrored), `startDirection`. Gizmos show the view box and the ledge ray when selected. Rigidbody/collider must sit on the root.
- `Dog` (built by `DogSetup`, **Tools > Jump and Run > Build Dog Prefab**): bark coroutine on the rising edge of `IsChasing` (sets `HoldPosition`), run animation (legs/tail) from the position change since the Rigidbody is kinematic, `OnStomped` → dizzy → turn +90° around Y → run along `-transform.forward` (lane "front" = camera side, also after the bend), height from `CurbHeight(run)` (curb 2 m in front of the lane, road 0.2 lower, parabola hop; Inspector fields `curb*`) → `SetActive(false)`. The `Woof Bubble` (TextMeshPro 3D + quads) and `Dizzy Stars` hang on the root, **not** on `Body`, so the text is never mirrored. Both are saved inactive in the prefab. Colors via MaterialPropertyBlock on `Dog.mat`. The model sits under `Body/Model`, shifted by `ModelCenterOffset` (0.23) so it lies centered around the root; otherwise the symmetric BoxCollider wouldn't match the look after a flip.
- **Level reset on respawn**: `GameManager.RespawnPlayer()` raises the static event `GameManager.LevelReset`. Subscribers (subscribe in `Awake`, unsubscribe in `OnDestroy`): `EnemyDamage` revives squashed enemies, `EnemyPlatformPatrol` and `PointToPointMover` go back to their start positions, `PowerUp` reactivates (`respawnOnLevelReset`), and `PlayerController` clears boosts, shine and platform momentum. Enemies and power-ups are therefore only **deactivated** (`SetActive(false)`), never `Destroy`ed. Keep it that way for new resettable objects.
- `PizzaCarton` (on `Pizza Carton`, child of the Player): mirrors its local X offset in `LateUpdate` based on `PlayerController.FacingDirection` (+1/-1, set from the world-space input direction, not from velocity, so platforms don't turn the player; it keeps its last value while standing still). Instant flip. It also controls the `steam` particle system: emission rate and start alpha × `Clamp01(HeatPercent)` (Inspector values = full heat).
- `GameManager` (singleton): `levelTime` = keep-warm time, `RemainingTime`/`HeatPercent` (can be > 1), `CoinsForHeat`, `TwoCoinHeat`/`ThreeCoinHeat`. `DeliverPizza()` (called by `LevelGoal`; returns false if the level is already over) and pizza going cold set `LevelFinished`, freeze the player (`PlayerController.Freeze()`) and raise the static events `PizzaDelivered(int coins)` / `PizzaCold`. While `LevelFinished` is true, `RespawnPlayer()` is ignored. `AddTime()` is intentionally not capped.
- `LanePath` (`[ExecuteAlways]`, `LanePath.Instance`) + static helper `Lane` (`Project`, `Point`, `Tangent`, `Back`, `Frame`). Built by `CityLevelBuilder` as the "Lane Path" object (start (−40, 0, 0), u0 −40, straight 104 → arc 90° → straight 110). Gizmo shows the path. Code that used to compare X positions now uses `Lane.Project(...)` (dogs) or flat distance (customer).
- `LevelGoal`: after a delivery it waits `continueInputDelay` (0.5 s), then for Jump (Space / gamepad South) and loads the next level. On the last level it only logs.
- `PizzaHud` (on the `HUD` prefab, uGUI + TextMeshPro): heat bar (width via `anchorMax.x`, color from gradient, overheat overlay for > 100%, ticks at the coin thresholds), `Delivered Panel` with 3 coins (built-in `Knob` sprite, popping in one by one), `Cold Panel`. Both panels are saved **inactive** in the prefab (otherwise they cover the Game view in edit mode). Display only, no GraphicRaycaster/EventSystem needed.
- `PizzaHeatSetup` (editor, **Tools > Jump and Run > Pizza Heat**): `Create HUD Prefab` (builds the HUD in code → `Assets/Prefabs/HUD.prefab`; needs the TMP Essential Resources), `Add HUD to Active Scene` (prefab instance + save scene), `Add Steam to Player Prefab` (particle system `Steam` under `Pizza Carton`, URP `ParticlesUnlit`, world simulation space). All re-runnable; existing objects get replaced. Changes to the HUD/steam layout belong in this tool, otherwise the next run overwrites them.
- `Customer` (on `Goal/Customer`, built by `CustomerSetup`, **Tools > Jump and Run > Build Customer into Goal Prefab**, which also builds the house via `CityPropsSetup.BuildCustomerHouse`): states Inside → ComingOut → Waiting → Receiving → Dancing (`homeDoor` = the house's SpawnDoor; its points are positioned for the customer's feet here, not for the player). On delivery while still Inside/ComingOut it gets placed outside immediately. `LevelReset` → back inside (`SpawnDoor.SnapClosed`). The Goal root sits at the platform center like all city prefabs (the trigger and customer are 0.5 higher; before that the customer was sunk 0.5 m into the sidewalk), listens to `PizzaDelivered`. Moves only `Rig` (the root stays put); arms swing via `Arm Pivot L/R` (shoulder pivot, rotation around Z: 0 = hanging, the -X arm goes up with negative angles; for waving, around X forward for holding). On handoff it disables `PizzaCarton` (otherwise LateUpdate would put it back) and reparents the carton to `Rig`; from then on `Customer.LateUpdate` places it every frame at `HoldPoint()` = the midpoint between both hand tips (`armLength` along the arm pivot) + a bit above/in front, rotation = rig. Dances only use **holding poses** (`SetHoldPose(lift)`: both arms rotate around X forward/up, slightly inward around Z), so the pizza always stays between the hands (3 coins: lifted up in front of the face; `holdForward` keeps it out of the head). Colors through a **MaterialPropertyBlock** on the shared `Customer.mat` (white), also live in edit mode via `OnValidate`. Material `Customer`. No colliders except the Goal trigger.
- `TrafficLane` (one per lane in `Street Crossing`): **deterministic** rhythm from a lane clock (`laneTime`, reset to 0 on `LevelReset`): car n starts at `n * interval` (+ `startOffset`) at `spawnZ` and is at `z = spawnZ - speed * (t - n*interval)`. **Object pool** (created once in `Awake` from the inactive child `Car Template`, `floor(travelTime/interval) + 2` cars); pool slot i drives the car numbers i, i+poolSize, ... Moved in `FixedUpdate` via `Rigidbody.MovePosition` (kinematic, interpolated), teleported to the back on a restart. Car height = `heightOverZ.Evaluate(z)` (AnimationCurve, linear tangents, set by `StreetSetup`; default flat 0). `Car`: BoxCollider **trigger** → `RespawnPlayer()` (like KillZone), color from a palette via MaterialPropertyBlock, picked deterministically from the car number (same colors after a respawn). `StreetSetup` (**Tools > Jump and Run > Build Street Crossing Prefab**) builds the prefab. Materials `Asphalt` (crosswalk), `Road Marking`, `Car`, `Wheel`, `Headlight` (Unlit); road/sidewalk/curb/foundation use the city materials (run Build City Prefabs first).
- **Respawn flow** (`GameManager.RespawnRoutine`): `IsRespawning` blocks further `RespawnPlayer()` calls → `PlayerLife.PlayDeath()` (kinematic, squash/spin, `poof.Emit`, invisible) → `LevelReset` → `PlayerLife.PlayExit(currentDoor)` (starts behind the door in the dark room, door opens, `MovePosition` to `OutsidePoint` on Z = 0, then `Revive`) or without a door `PlayPopIn(currentRespawnPoint)` (level 2). Level start: `startDoor` on GameManager → `PlayExit`. `PlayerController` does nothing in `FixedUpdate` while the Rigidbody is kinematic; `Freeze()`/`Unfreeze()`; `ResetLook` also resets `body.rotation` (otherwise the Rigidbody writes the spun rotation back).
- `SpawnDoor` (on `Pizzeria`/`Coffee Shop`): `insidePoint`, `outsidePoint`, `doorLeaf` (hinge pivot, opens inward by -100° around Y). `Checkpoint` has an optional `door` → `GameManager.SetCheckpoint(SpawnDoor)`; without one, the old Transform respawn point. - `MicrowaveStation` (on the `Kiosk` city prefab, trigger in front of the counter): sequence via `PlayerLife.BeginScripted/WalkTo/EndScripted` (kinematic like the respawn), sets `GameManager.InCutscene` (blocks `RespawnPlayer`), disables `PizzaCarton` and reparents the carton into `Carton Spot` (rotates as the turntable), `GameManager.ReheatPizza()` at the ding. `GameManager.ElapsedTime` = stopwatch (stops at `LevelFinished`), shown by `PizzaHud` (`stopwatchText`, `deliveredTimeText`, `PizzaHud.FormatTime`).
- `BounceAwning`: on `OnCollisionEnter` from above (player's underside ≥ top edge − tolerance) → `PlayerController.Bounce(20)` (no jump cut while rising, `ignoreJumpCut`) + squashes the `Canvas`.
- **City tools** (**Tools > Jump and Run > City**): `CityPropsSetup` (`Build City Prefabs` → `Assets/Prefabs/City/*`, materials in `Assets/Materials/City/`; `Add Death Animation to Player Prefab` → `PlayerLife` + `Poof`). `CityLevelBuilder` (`Rebuild Level 1 (City)`; all positions in u along the LanePath; creates the lane, a corner plaza under the curve (non-prefab boxes: sidewalk slab with collider + asphalt), two building rows (before the bend up to the coffee shop, after the bend from u = arc end + 7, where the row comes out behind the coffee shop); reserves the kiosk u 77–83 and the customer house 142–150; the row after the bend runs to u 170 so its backs line the side street; `BuildSideStreet` adds a house row on the left of the cross street with facades facing it (rotated −90°, **unpacked** from their prefabs, otherwise `PrefabGridEnforcer` would turn them back into the lane frame) plus paving strips (`Side Street Ground`)) **deletes level 1 completely** except camera, light, Player, GameManager, HUD, KillZone, Goal, StartPoint and rebuilds it (sidewalk/pit lengths via root `scale.x`). ## Pitfalls
- **Kinematic Rigidbodies moved by script** (dogs, cars) need `constraints = None`. The old Enemy prefab had FreezePositionZ + FreezeRotation from the 2D era; after the bend (lane along Z) that locked the dogs in place.
- `LanePath` has `[DefaultExecutionOrder(-100)]`, so `Lane.*` works in other scripts' `Awake`. Without it, the order was random and scripts got the X-axis fallback (dogs after the bend started at the wrong u).
- Jump reach limits level design. Right now that's about 3.2 m high and about 5.5 m far at `moveSpeed` 7. If you change the jump or moveSpeed, check the gaps in `CityLevelBuilder` (level 1: pits 3.5 m, balconies 3 m higher each, 2–2.5 m gaps over the long pit) and level 2 (5.5 m gaps with a 1 m step up).
- The player collider gets a **zero-friction PhysicsMaterial** in `Awake`. With the strong custom gravity, default friction (0.6) almost cancelled out walking acceleration. Don't remove it.
- "Grounded" for jumping = ground check hit + no jump in the last 0.1 s (`GroundLockAfterJump`). **Don't** add a vertical-velocity condition. On moving platforms the player's vy never exactly matches the platform's (easing, solver), so jumps got dropped.
- The player Rigidbody needs **Interpolate** (set on the prefab). Without it the camera jitters, because physics runs at 50 Hz and rendering doesn't.
- **`PrefabGridEnforcer` snaps every prefab root to whole meters** (X/Y, Z = 0), including positions set from code or the MCP. Convention: a prefab root sits on the **center of a 1 m thick platform** (whole number), and content that should sit on the surface is shifted 0.5 up inside the prefab (see `Street Crossing`, all city prefabs). Anything that should be further back (facades Z = 3, skyline Z = 30, clouds Z = 70) is also shifted inside the prefab; the root stays at Z = 0. Objects on a platform (dogs) have their root 1 m above the platform center. Prefabs stretched via `scale.x` (sidewalk, pit) need their center on a whole meter, so pick their length to match.
- Serialized values in prefabs/scenes override code defaults. If you change a default in code, also check the prefab (`Assets/Prefabs/Player.prefab`) and scene overrides.
- New `.cs` files aren't in `Assembly-CSharp.csproj` until Unity re-imports (the csproj lists every file). For a pre-check without the editor, add a `<Compile Include=...>` line by hand.
- Verify it compiles without the editor: `/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.6.2f1/Unity.app/Contents/Resources/Scripting/DotNetSdk/dotnet build Assembly-CSharp.csproj` (the CS0649 warnings for `[SerializeField]` are normal). Game feel can only be tested in the editor, so ask the user.

- **City houses must not be "Batching Static"**: static batching merges the cubes into one mesh, then the facade shader sees an identity object matrix and every house looks the same (and has no windows). The SRP Batcher handles the ~200 houses with one material fine.
- **Render settings (fog, sky, ambient, sun) come from the active scene.** With City + level loaded together, the City must be the active scene, and the level scenes must not bring their own directional light.
- **The game camera barely sees above the horizon**: it looks down ~27° (`offset` (0,5,−8), `lookHeight` 1) with a 60° vertical FOV, so the top edge of the frame is ~3° above horizontal, and the house row directly behind the lane blocks the rest. Tall background things (monsters, tower, mountains) only show up from open spots or other camera angles.
- New text on TextMeshPro 3D objects (e.g. street signs) adds glyphs to the dynamic atlas in `LiberationSans SDF.asset`; commit that change with it.
