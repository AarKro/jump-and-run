# Jump and Run

A small 2.5D platformer the user is building to **learn Unity**. When you change code, explain the Unity idea behind it (for example interpolation or why physics runs in FixedUpdate). Don't just make silent fixes.

## Working mode
- **Never assume. Always ask, interview style.** Before you implement or decide anything (design, gameplay, code structure, naming, assets), ask the user with a set of concrete options (use the AskUserQuestion tool). Put the option you **recommend first** and mark it as recommended. Only act once the user has chosen.
- **Git**: local repo (branch `main`, no remote, no LFS yet; set up LFS before the first large binary like models/textures/audio). **Commit after every finished work item**, as soon as it compiles cleanly and is done on your side, without waiting for the playtest. Tuning or fixes after the playtest get their own small commits. Commit messages in **English**, short and imperative. `.gitignore` covers `Library/`, `Temp/`, `Logs/`, `UserSettings/`, `obj/`, `*.csproj`, `*.slnx`, `.DS_Store`.

## Theme (decided as of 2026-10-23)
Only decided things go here, no loose ideas.
- **Pizza delivery**: the player is a pizza delivery guy carrying a pizza carton. Each level he picks up a new pizza at his **pizzeria** (start, a house in the background) and delivers it to a **customer** (goal), who is super happy and starts dancing.
- **Level 1 = city.** Enemies are dogs (instead of the red `Enemy`). Platforms are city objects (benches, balconies, ...).
- **Heat meter + tip**: the pizza cools down over time (a heat bar at the top center, no seconds shown). The remaining heat on delivery sets the tip as **1–3 gold coins** (heat thirds: ≥ 2/3 = 3, ≥ 1/3 = 2, > 0 = 1). **The heat bar explains itself** (decided 2026-10-24): pizza icon at its left end, under each third the coins it is worth (1, 2, 3); when the heat drops below a third, that coin group turns grey and shakes (pops back if a chili lifts it again). At every level start (also after restarts) "Keep the pizza hot!" shows under the bar for ~3 s. No sound yet (the project has no audio). **No timer/stopwatch anywhere** (removed 2026-10-24: it suggested a link to the heat bar); heat is the only score. **HUD contrast**: every HUD element sits on a dark rounded plate (92% opaque, because the project blends UI in linear color space, where 82% looked like 50%): heat plate (icon + bar + coins), hint pill, Delivered card, Cold card, intro title plate. At 0: "Pizza's cold!" for 1.5 s, then the level restarts. Respawning costs no extra heat (the lost time is the penalty). **Chili pickups** (the only power-up, decided 2026-10-24; speed/jump boosts were removed) warm the pizza back up by **1/6 of the bar** (half a coin segment) and **may go above 100%** (buffer, shown as a pulsing overlay on the bar). Floating glowing red chili, **upright** (stem on top), sways ±30° (no full spin, otherwise it reads as a red blob), small flames; on pickup: pop + ember burst + steam puff from the carton + a flame flies from the chili into the heat bar (the bar only shows the new heat when it lands, then flashes). **One-time**: they don't come back after a respawn (no heat farming by dying), only on a level restart. 3 per level on the old power-up spots (level 2 only has 2 since 2026-10-24, the lifeguard tower's chili was removed: too many power-ups). Keep-warm time: level 1 = 60 s, level 2 = 80 s (scene overrides on `levelTime`, still to be tuned).
- **Delivery**: player frozen (input off, gravity/platform keep going), panel "Delivered!" + coins, Jump continues to the next level.
- **Steam** on the carton fades evenly with the heat.
- **Customer** (goal): lives in their own **customer house** (house no. 42, part of the Goal prefab, integrated into the row of buildings). They wait inside and come out of the door once the player is < 8 m away, then sway and wave (< 6 m). After a respawn they go back inside (unless the pizza was already delivered). On delivery the carton hops over in an arc into their hands, then they dance **by tip**: 1 coin = sway, 2 = bounce + alternating arms, 3 = hop + spin + arms overhead. Shirt/skin color per level in the Inspector. The "Delivered!" panel is compact at the top (no darkening), so the dance stays visible.
- **Dog**: walks back and forth (1.7 m/s) and turns at ledges and walls; **never falls off a ledge** (when chasing it stops there). It **sees** the player only in its viewing direction (6 m, ±1.5 m height, free line of sight); sneaking up from behind works. Once spotted: stop, "WOOF!" bubble + hop 0.5 s (warning, again every time it spots you), then charges at 5 m/s (player 7; at 6 you landed on its head almost every time you jumped past). While chasing it keeps the player in view in all directions and gives up after 1.5 s without sight. **Turning around takes time** (decided 2026-10-24, so jumping over a chasing dog gives a head start instead of it landing under you): brake (25 m/s²), turn in place 0.3 s (the body yaws 180° through the camera side), accelerate again (20 m/s²), ~0.5 s in total; patrol turns at ledges/walls use the same turn. It doesn't spot the player while turning. Fur/ear colors per dog in the Inspector.
- **Street with cars**: `Street Crossing` is an overlay on a sidewalk, a **real side street** in a 10 m gap of the building row: road at road level (0.3) with sidewalks + curbs on both sides up to z = 104 (beyond that the city's Anchovy Alley continues), crossing the main road in front (yellow line covered), and a **raised crosswalk** at sidewalk height (0.5) with 1.5 m ramps on the walk lane, so the lane stays flat. Cars come in a fixed rhythm from the background (+Z, spawn at z 310 at the end of Anchovy Alley; was 40, +270 m = exactly 9 intervals, so the rhythm at the crosswalk is unchanged) toward the camera and roll over the bump (`heightOverZ` curve); the warning is that you see them coming. Default 12 m/s, every 2.5 s, lane 2 offset by 1.25 s. **The whole car is deadly** (respawn), jumping all the way over it is allowed. On respawn every lane starts its rhythm from the beginning (street pre-filled with cars). In level 1 at u = 50 (`CityLevelBuilder.StreetU`).
- In-game texts in **English**.
- **Level 1 = city street with a 90° bend after the coffee shop** (as of 2026-10-23, generated by `CityLevelBuilder`, about 170 m, keep-warm time 75 s): sidewalk with road and curb in front, buildings behind (facades at Z = 3; since the move into the city they are the **city's** houses, plus the city's pizzeria/coffee shop/kiosk), clouds. Gaps = **construction pits** (4 m deep, the KillZone sits right above the floor at y = -3, so the death is still visible). Order: pizzeria (start) → balcony climb + chili → pit → dog between two dumpsters → cross street with traffic → **coffee shop = checkpoint** → bouncy awning → high balcony with chili → long pit over the window-washer gondola and two balconies → 2 dogs (bench/dumpsters as turning walls) + balcony with chili → customer. **No microwave in level 1** (the kiosk is only scenery there; the microwave comes back in level 4).
- **Start/respawn from the door**: at the level start the delivery guy walks out of the pizzeria. **Death** (dog, car, pit; reworked 2026-10-24): freeze, he falls stiffly **onto his face** (arms stretched forward), the carton flies forward out of his hands in an arc, flips and the lid pops open, the **pizza spins out and lands cheese-side down** with a tomato/cheese splat; he lies there ~0.8 s, then the **circle iris closes on him**; the level resets in the black, the camera snaps to the door, the iris opens there and he walks out of the door of the last building (pizzeria, or coffee shop after the checkpoint). Further hits during that time don't count.
- **Checkpoint feedback (coffee shop, gelato shop)**: before = shop dark (windows off, sign dim, "CLOSED" on the door and on the sidewalk chalkboard). No floating hint props (cup/cone removed 2026-10-24: walking past and seeing it change is enough). When reached: lights on, the "OPEN" neon flickers on, the chalkboard flips to "OPEN – fresh coffee", and a 3D text **"Checkpoint unlocked!"** pops up above the shop sign with sparkles, rises and fades (~2 s). No HUD toast.
- **Microwave** (24/7 `Kiosk` prefab; **removed from the city and level 1 on 2026-10-24, comes back in level 4** at the kiosk checkpoint on Marinara Promenade): walking into it automatically walks the player to the counter, the carton goes into the microwave, it runs (glowing glass, countdown, turntable), "DING!", carton out, back onto the lane, **heat back to 100%** (a buffer above that stays). **Once per level** (decided 2026-10-24 for level 4: display READY → USED, stays USED after respawns, only a 'Pizza's cold!' restart makes it READY again; once per life would let port deaths refill the pizza, since the kiosk checkpoint sits right before it).
- **Bouncy awning**: shop awning as a trampoline (~5 m high, independent of the jump button). The window-washer gondola works as an elevator (6 m).
- **Dog stomp**: no squashing. The player still bounces, the dog gets dizzy (stars over its head, 1 s), runs toward the camera (−Z, 10 m, small hop down the curb onto the road) out of the bottom of the screen and disappears, then comes back on `LevelReset`.
- **Player look (built 2026-10-24)**: delivery guy made of primitives, **same size as the customer (~1.6 m)**, pizzeria brand colors: red polo with white chest logo, red cap with white brim, dark pants, white sneakers, brown hair. Carries the red carton with a white lid (`Pizza Carton` → `Box`/`Lid`, materials `PizzaRed`/`PizzaWhite`, no colliders) **with both hands in front of the belly**. Turns **fully** into the walking direction (±90°, pure profile; the user wanted a clean turn, 3/4 looked unfinished), faces into the depth when walking out of a door. Hitbox = **capsule** (radius 0.5, height 1.6, center y 0.3, so the feet stay at y −0.5 like the old sphere's bottom). Tested: the level-1 balcony climbs still work (jump ~2 m before the balcony edge).

## Gameplay structure
- **2.5D on a lane path**: the walk lane is a `LanePath` (straight pieces + arcs in the XZ plane, lane coordinate **u**; on the first straight piece along +X, u = world X). **Level 1 bends 90° into the depth after the coffee shop** (left turn toward +Z, radius 4 m, u 64–70.3). Lane frame at u: local +X = walking direction, +Z = "back" (building side), −Z = camera side; `Lane.Frame(u)`. All city prefabs are built in this frame and placed rotated. **Without a LanePath (level 2), `Lane` behaves exactly like the old X-axis at Z = 0.** Player rotation is frozen for physics (constraints 112), position Z is **no longer** frozen (the lane holds it). `PrefabGridEnforcer` (editor) snaps prefab roots **along the lane** (u and y to whole meters, rotation = lane frame; UI/RectTransforms excluded; without a lane, the old X/Y grid at Z = 0).
- `PlayerController`: Rigidbody movement (velocity set directly). **Input x = forward/back along the lane** (W/S don't move), **mirrored when the camera looks from the other side of the lane** (`inputSign` from `Camera.main.right` vs. the lane tangent, latched when a direction is pressed and kept while it's held, Mario-64 style, so a camera flip mid-run doesn't turn him around); each `FixedUpdate` `SnapToLane(u)` projects the player back onto the path (sideways correction) and sets `body.rotation = Lane.Frame(u)` (so the carton sits on the correct side in local X); sideways velocity is dropped.  Running: no instant start speed anymore (`startSpeedFraction` removed 2026-10-24, playtesters found it hectic): speeding up uses `acceleration` (30 → full 7 m/s in ~0.25 s), braking (release, over speed, turning around) uses `deceleration` (150); when turning he brakes to a stop first, then accelerates. Celeste/Hollow-Knight-style jump: gravity derived from `jumpHeight`/`timeToApex`, stronger gravity when falling, jump cut on release, apex hang, coyote time and jump buffer. `IsGrounded` is public (for the model animation).
- `ThirdPersonCamera`: follows the player in `LateUpdate`; anchored at the **lane point** at the player's u (with his height), not his full position, so it stays put while he is behind a door or at the microwave; offset (0, 5, −8), `lookHeight` 2.86 (~15° down) and viewing angle **in the lane frame at the player's position** (in a curve it swings around the outside), separate SmoothDamp for horizontal/vertical, rotation smoothed with `rotationSharpness` (exponential).
- `PointToPointMover`: the shared A↔B movement (used directly by the **Flying Enemy** prefab). `MovingPlatform` is an empty subclass of it, so the player recognizes platforms. Movement details:
- `MovingPlatform`: kinematic Rigidbody, moves A↔B with ease in/out (`easingStrength`, peak speed = `speed` × strength; `speed` = average speed), waits `waitTime` at each end, starts from its editor position (projected onto A→B, heading toward B first), and only waits at start if it sits on an endpoint. A/B are children of the platform (`moving platform start/end`) and their positions are read once in `Start`. It does **not** move passengers itself; it exposes `Velocity`, and `PlayerController` adds that velocity to its own (`carryVelocity` stays while airborne, so jumps keep platform momentum; rising platforms add their upward speed to the jump). Never move the dynamic player with `MovePosition`/teleporting, because that fights the physics and kills jumps.
- **Player model** (`PlayerModelSetup`, **Tools > Jump and Run > Build Player Model**, rebuilds into `Player.prefab`, materials in `Assets/Materials/Player/`): `Model` (yaw pivot) → `Rig` (bob) → body parts, `Leg Pivot L/R` (hip), `Arm Pivot L/R` (shoulder), `Carton Hold` (between the hands). The model faces −Z (camera) at yaw 0, like the customer. Also swaps the root collider to the capsule. `DeliveryGuy` (on the Player root) animates it **procedurally** in `Update`: speed from the position change (works while kinematic, e.g. walking out of the door), yaw = −60° × `FacingDirection` or toward the movement when walking into the depth, leg swing with phase driven by distance (`strideLength`), bob, jump pose when not grounded (kinematic counts as grounded), arms hold the carton while `PizzaCarton.enabled`, otherwise they hang and swing. `PizzaCarton.holdPoint` = `Carton Hold`: the carton follows it in `LateUpdate` (without a hold point, the old left/right mirroring is used). Death squash/spin works because `PlayerLife` transforms the root.
- Prefabs are capitalized (`Platform Big`, `Moving Platform Small`, `Dog`, `Checkpoint`, `Goal`, `Heat Pickup`, ...). Paths in editor scripts must match exactly.
- Enemy prefabs: `Dog` (formerly `Enemy`, renamed with the same GUID; `GroundPatrol` + `Dog`, BoxCollider 1.25 × 0.89 m) and `Flying Enemy` (purple, `PointToPointMover` between its children `Point A`/`Point B`). Both use `EnemyDamage`.
- `EnemyDamage`: a contact from above (direction to player `y >= stompDirectionThreshold`, player not rising) is a **stomp**. Stomp is decided **by height**: the player's underside ≥ the enemy collider's top edge − `stompHeightTolerance` (0.2), for all enemies. The enemy is disabled immediately (collider plus all other scripts), and `PlayerController.StompBounce()` bounces the player (higher while jump is held). If the enemy has a component with **`IStompHandler`** (e.g. `Dog`), it stays enabled and decides the reaction itself in `OnStomped()` (and has to call `SetActive(false)` at the end); otherwise it squashes flat. Any other contact calls `GameManager.RespawnPlayer()`.
- `GroundPatrol` (replaces the old waypoint patrol `EnemyPlatformPatrol`; the platform prefabs no longer have `WaypointA/B` children): kinematic Rigidbody, moves **along the lane** (own `u`, fixed height, `MovePosition(Lane.Point(u))` + `MoveRotation(Lane.Frame(u))`, so also through curves). Ledge check = raycast down at `Lane.Point(u ± (half body length + lookahead))` (body length from the BoxCollider size, not from the axis-aligned bounds), wall check = raycast along the tangent. Vision: distance **along the lane** (`sightRange`) + height (`sightHeight`), viewing direction only to spot, `Physics.Linecast` against `sightBlockers` for line of sight, `loseSightTime`. Speed ramps with `acceleration`/`deceleration`; a direction change brakes to 0, then `turnDuration` turns in place (logic in `FixedUpdate`, the yaw of `flipTarget` is animated in `Update` with `Time.time - Time.fixedTime` added so it's smooth between physics steps; at 180° `SetFacing` swaps to the mirrored scale, which looks identical because the dog is left/right symmetric). Hooks: `IsChasing`, `HoldPosition` (stand still, turn toward the player), `FacingDirection`, `flipTarget` (for the dog `Body`, so collider/Rigidbody stay unmirrored), `startDirection`. Gizmos show the view box and the ledge ray when selected. Rigidbody/collider must sit on the root.
- `Dog` (built by `DogSetup`, **Tools > Jump and Run > Build Dog Prefab**): bark coroutine on the rising edge of `IsChasing` (sets `HoldPosition`), run animation (legs/tail) from the position change since the Rigidbody is kinematic, `OnStomped` → dizzy → turn +90° around Y → run along `-transform.forward` (lane "front" = camera side, also after the bend), height from `CurbHeight(run)` (curb 2 m in front of the lane, road 0.2 lower, parabola hop; Inspector fields `curb*`) → `SetActive(false)`. The `Woof Bubble` (TextMeshPro 3D + quads) and `Dizzy Stars` hang on the root, **not** on `Body`, so the text is never mirrored. Both are saved inactive in the prefab. Colors via MaterialPropertyBlock on `Dog.mat`. The model sits under `Body/Model`, shifted by `ModelCenterOffset` (0.23) so it lies centered around the root; otherwise the symmetric BoxCollider wouldn't match the look after a flip.
- **Level reset on respawn**: `GameManager.RespawnPlayer()` raises the static event `GameManager.LevelReset`. Subscribers (subscribe in `Awake`, unsubscribe in `OnDestroy`): `EnemyDamage` revives squashed enemies, `EnemyPlatformPatrol` and `PointToPointMover` go back to their start positions, `HeatPickup` reactivates only if `respawnOnLevelReset` (off for chilis), and `PlayerController` clears platform momentum and buffered input. Enemies and pickups are therefore only **deactivated** (`SetActive(false)`), never `Destroy`ed. Keep it that way for new resettable objects.
- `PizzaCarton` (on `Pizza Carton`, child of the Player): mirrors its local X offset in `LateUpdate` based on `PlayerController.FacingDirection` (+1/-1, set from the world-space input direction, not from velocity, so platforms don't turn the player; it keeps its last value while standing still). Instant flip. It also controls the `steam` particle system: emission rate and start alpha × `Clamp01(HeatPercent)` (Inspector values = full heat).

## Pitfalls
- **Kinematic Rigidbodies moved by script** (dogs, cars) need `constraints = None`. The old Enemy prefab had FreezePositionZ + FreezeRotation from the 2D era; after the bend (lane along Z) that locked the dogs in place.
- `LanePath` has `[DefaultExecutionOrder(-100)]`, so `Lane.*` works in other scripts' `Awake`. Without it, the order was random and scripts got the X-axis fallback (dogs after the bend started at the wrong u).
- Jump reach limits level design. Right now that's about 3.2 m high and about 5.5 m far at `moveSpeed` 7. If you change the jump or moveSpeed, check the gaps in `CityLevelBuilder` (level 1: pits 3.5 m, balconies 3 m higher each, 2–2.5 m gaps over the long pit) and level 2 (5.5 m gaps with a 1 m step up).
- The player collider gets a **zero-friction PhysicsMaterial** in `Awake`. With the strong custom gravity, default friction (0.6) almost cancelled out walking acceleration. Don't remove it.
- "Grounded" for jumping = ground check hit + no jump in the last 0.1 s (`GroundLockAfterJump`). **Don't** add a vertical-velocity condition. On moving platforms the player's vy never exactly matches the platform's (easing, solver), so jumps got dropped.
- The player Rigidbody needs **Interpolate** (set on the prefab). Without it the camera jitters, because physics runs at 50 Hz and rendering doesn't.
- **`PrefabGridEnforcer` snaps every prefab root to whole meters** (X/Y, Z = 0), including positions set from code or the MCP. Convention: a prefab root sits on the **center of a 1 m thick platform** (whole number), and content that should sit on the surface is shifted 0.5 up inside the prefab (see `Street Crossing`, all city prefabs). Anything that should be further back (facades Z = 3, skyline Z = 30, clouds Z = 70) is also shifted inside the prefab; the root stays at Z = 0. Objects on a platform (dogs) have their root 1 m above the platform center. Prefabs stretched via `scale.x` (sidewalk, pit) need their center on a whole meter, so pick their length to match.
- Serialized values in prefabs/scenes override code defaults. If you change a default in code, also check the prefab (`Assets/Prefabs/Player.prefab`) and scene overrides.
- New `.cs` files aren't in `Assembly-CSharp.csproj` until Unity re-imports (the csproj lists every file). For a pre-check without the editor, add a `<Compile Include=...>` line by hand.
- Verify it compiles without the editor: `/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.6.2f1/Unity.app/Contents/Resources/Scripting/DotNetSdk/dotnet build Assembly-CSharp.csproj` (the CS0649 warnings for `[SerializeField]` are normal). Game feel can only be tested in the editor, so ask the user.

- **City houses must not be "Batching Static"**: static batching merges the cubes into one mesh, then the facade shader sees an identity object matrix and every house looks the same (and has no windows). The SRP Batcher handles the ~200 houses with one material fine.
- **Render settings (fog, sky, ambient, sun) come from the active scene.** With City + level loaded together, the City must be the active scene, and the level scenes must not bring their own directional light.
- **The game camera barely sees above the horizon**: it looks down ~27° (`offset` (0,5,−8), `lookHeight` 1) with a 60° vertical FOV, so the top edge of the frame is ~3° above horizontal, and the house row directly behind the lane blocks the rest. Tall background things (monsters, tower, mountains) only show up from open spots or other camera angles.
- With Domain Reload off, **static fields keep their values between Play sessions**. Reset them in a `[RuntimeInitializeOnLoadMethod(RuntimeInitializeLoadType.SubsystemRegistration)]` method (see `LevelFlow.ResetStatics`). Recompiling during Play mode also wipes non-serialized fields without running `Awake` again; create such helpers lazily (`KaijuFight.visorBlock`).
- **`Mathf.SmoothStep(from, to, t)` is not shader `smoothstep(edge0, edge1, x)`**: it interpolates between from and to. For "0 until 0.8, then smoothly to 1" write `Mathf.SmoothStep(0f, 1f, Mathf.InverseLerp(0.8f, 1f, k))` (mixing them up shrank the flying duck to 13 % right after take-off).
- **`CityGround` must be axis-aligned boxes**: `LevelCity.Carve` rebuilds cut slabs as axis-aligned cubes, so a rotated slab (like the beach slope) must not be `CityGround`. Patterns on cuttable ground belong in a world-space shader (Paving), not in UVs or many small tile objects.
- **Level ground must match the city's road cross-section**: the ring roads are 10 m wide (curb at −2, road to −12, center line at −7 in the lane frame, `CityLayout.RingOffset`). The `Sidewalk` prefab, level 1's corner plaza, the `Street Crossing` intersection patch and the level-1 cutouts use exactly that; with the old 12 m road, edge and center line jumped where level ground meets the city road. Cutouts must end exactly where the level ground ends (computed from the lane), otherwise the grass below shows through the gap.
- **`CityCutout` only hides city objects whose bounds center lies inside the box**: a house that is half inside stays. For a lot that needs to be fully free, reserve it in `CityLayout.LandmarkLots` instead (the generator lays houses around it; each block has its own random seed, so only that block's houses change).
- **Coplanar faces flicker (Z-fighting)**: an added wall exactly on a cut edge of the city ground fights with it. Offset such pieces slightly (pit walls sit 0.1 m inside the gap). Same for tops: the `Construction Pit` back wall ends 1 cm below the sidewalk surface, because the pit is 1 m wider than the gap and its ends sit inside the sidewalks.
- In MCP play tests, teleports must land **inside** triggers (checkpoint trigger in front of the door, goal trigger ~2 m wide); being 1 m off silently does nothing.
- City ground under a level must be cuttable everywhere it shows through: road, sidewalks, lines, **and** the sea/land/beach slabs below the city (all `CityGround`); otherwise pits show water or grass.
- **Unity can't serialize references between scenes** (level → city object). Look such things up at runtime (`CityLandmark.Find(id)`) and hand them over (`GameManager.SetStartDoor`).
- **Trigger/collision messages also reach disabled MonoBehaviours.** To switch off a trigger-based feature (checkpoint, microwave), disable the trigger **collider**, not the script (`CityLandmark.SetUsed`).
- City state survives level restarts and swaps (only the level scene reloads). Anything stateful in the city (checkpoint reached, coffee shop open, microwave used) must be reset by `CityLandmark.SetUsed` when a level starts.
- New text on TextMeshPro 3D objects (e.g. street signs) adds glyphs to the dynamic atlas in `LiberationSans SDF.asset`; commit that change with it.
